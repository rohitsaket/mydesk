   313→# 9. ATTENDANCE CALCULATION ENGINE
   314→
   315→Track:
   316→
   317→```text
   318→scheduled_duration
   319→gross_work_duration
   320→net_work_duration
   321→break_duration
   322→paid_break_duration
   323→unpaid_break_duration
   324→late_duration
   325→early_exit_duration
   326→overtime_duration
   327→productive_duration
   328→```
   329→
   330→Formula example:
   331→
   332→```text
   333→gross duration =
   334→last activity - first check-in
   335→
   336→net work =
   337→gross duration - unpaid breaks
   338→
   339→remaining =
   340→required work duration - net work
   341→```
   342→
   343→Never calculate payroll-sensitive totals only in the frontend.
   344→
   345→Backend remains authoritative.
